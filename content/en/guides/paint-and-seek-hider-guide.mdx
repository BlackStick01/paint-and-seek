## Understanding the Game Mechanics

Before you can excel as a hider, it's essential to understand the core mechanics of "Paint and Seek." The game features a unique map that resembles a maze, complete with locked doors, starry night skies, and children's drawings. Players can choose between two teams: hiders and seekers. As a hider, your goal is to survive for four minutes or escape the game, while seekers must kill at least one hider within three minutes and 30 seconds.

## Mastering the Hide and Seek Experience

When you enter the game, you'll be equipped with either a hider vest or seeker vest. The vests can be traded, but the swap must occur before player guards enter the room or the last player enters the game gate. This means you need to be quick on your feet and make strategic decisions to secure the vest that will benefit you the most.

## Choosing Your Starting Position

As a hider, you'll have a 30-second head start before the seekers spawn. Use this time wisely to find a hiding spot that's both secure and challenging for seekers to find. The map's layout and the various elements, such as locked doors and keyholes, can help you strategize your hideout.

## Dodging Techniques

One of the key abilities of a hider is dodging. Dodging a seeker's attack three times will cause them to be stunned, giving you time to escape. If you manage to dodge six consecutive attacks, you'll enter a quick-time event (QTE) minigame, where you can potentially steal the seeker's knife and gain an advantage.

## Navigating the Map

The map is a maze-like arena, filled with stars, children's drawings, and various obstacles. Some doors lead to pits, which can be used to trap seekers, while others contain keyholes that, when opened, can lead to an exit. Familiarize yourself with the map's layout to find the best hiding spots and to know where to find the exits and keys.

## Power-ups and Abilities

As a hider, you'll receive a dodge ability, which is crucial for survival. Additionally, every 20 seconds, a random key will spawn on the map, which you can collect to gain access to the exit doors. Keep an eye out for these keys, as they can be the difference between winning and losing.

## Community Tips

Player experience has shown that barricading doors can be a significant advantage. If a door is closed and you're on either side, you can barricade it, causing seekers to bang against it, giving you more time to escape. Additionally, remember that the game has a 20-second kill-sharing mechanic, which means if you want to share a kill, you must have gotten a hit within 20 seconds before the hider's death.

## The Role of the Seeker

As a hider, understanding the seeker's perspective can help you plan your strategies. Seekers can push their teammates into pits to obtain kills, and they will have knives to attack with. Dodging their attacks and finding creative hiding spots will be key to your survival.
