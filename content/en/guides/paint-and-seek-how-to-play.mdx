## What is Paint and Seek?

Paint and Seek is a unique multiplayer game where players must paint their bodies to blend in with their surroundings. Unlike traditional hide-and-seek, where players seek to stay hidden, Paint and Seek challenges players to create the perfect camouflage. The game is available on Steam and has already sold over a million copies in just four days, proving its appeal to players seeking a new and engaging experience.

## Gameplay Mechanics

1. **Choose Your Color**: At the start of the game, you'll choose a color to paint your body. The color must match the background of the level you're playing in.
2. **Paint Your Body**: Use the color wheel to paint your body to blend in with the environment.
3. **Find the Seeker**: The seeker's goal is to find all the players. The seekers have a color wheel to identify players by their colors.
4. **Seek and Hide**: Players must find a place to hide without being seen by the seeker.
5. **Win the Game**: The last player remaining wins the game.

## Strategies for Success

## Mastering Camouflage

- **Understand the Environment**: Study the level and identify areas that can be painted to create effective camouflage.
- **Experiment with Colors**: Don't be afraid to try different colors to see what works best in your environment.

## Outsmarting the Seeker

- **Use Shadows**: Hide in areas with shadows to make it harder for the seeker to spot you.
- **Stay Still**: When you're in a hiding spot, try to stay as still as possible to avoid being detected.
- **Know the Seeker's Color**: If you know the seeker's color, you can avoid painting your body in that color.
