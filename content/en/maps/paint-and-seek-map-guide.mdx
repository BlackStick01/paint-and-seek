## Tagging and Escaping

To win at Paint and Seek, you need to master the art of tagging and escaping. Here's a breakdown of the process:

- **Tagging:** When you find an opponent, you can tag them by painting their location with your paint. The number of tags required to win varies depending on the map.
- **Escaping:** Once you've tagged the required number of opponents, you must escape the map by finding a safe zone. Be aware that other players can tag you as you attempt to escape.

## Expert Tips for Winning Paint and Seek Maps

1. **Stay Mobile:** Always be on the move. Don't linger in one spot for too long, as this makes you an easy target.
2. **Utilize Landmarks:** Familiarize yourself with the map's landmarks and use them to your advantage. They can serve as both strategic points and hiding spots.
3. **Play Smart:** If you're outnumbered, consider tagging the weaker opponents first. This can help you gain the upper hand.
4. **Watch for Trails:** Players leave a trail when they move, so keep an eye out for these signs to track down your opponents.
5. **Learn from Player Experience:** Pay attention to the strategies used by other players in the community. This can provide valuable insights into winning tactics.
