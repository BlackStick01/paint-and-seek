## Understanding Paint and Seek Controls

Paint and Seek employs standard Roblox movement controls, but with a few game-specific additions. Here's a breakdown of the essential controls you'll need to know:

| Action | Key / Input |
| --- | --- |
| Move Forward / Back / Left / Right | W / A / S / D |
| Jump | Spacebar |
| Camera | Hold Right Mouse + Move Mouse |
| Interact / Tag | Left Mouse Button |
| Crouch / Prone | C |

These controls are fundamental for both Seekers and Hiders. Seekers will use movement to navigate the maps and search for Hiders, while Hiders will rely on crouching and prone positions to remain undetected.

## Essential Tips for Seekers

Seekers must be quick and observant to catch Hiders. Here are some tips to help you excel:

- **Learn the Maps**: Familiarize yourself with the four launch maps ? House, Grocery Store, Bank, and Arcade. Each map has unique hiding spots and seeker sweep routes that can give you an edge.
- **Use the Camera**: A good camera angle can make all the difference. Practice holding the right mouse button and moving the mouse to control the camera effectively.
- **Stay Quiet**: Avoid unnecessary movements or sounds that could alert Hiders to your presence.

## Essential Tips for Hiders

Hiders need to blend in and avoid detection. Here are some tips to help you stay undetected:

- **Sample Colors**: Use the eyedropper tool to sample the exact surface color and adjust the HSV and roughness to match the lighting. This will help you blend into the environment.
- **Choose the Right Pose**: Picking the right pose is crucial. Stay completely still once prep time ends to avoid detection.
- **Stay Informed**: Keep an eye on the community for updates on new maps, items, and strategies.
